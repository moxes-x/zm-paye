The rules

How it's worked out

Three deductions come off your pay. Each one uses a different base, which is why payslips can be confusing.

PAYE on gross pay

Income tax, charged in bands. Only the part of your pay inside each band is taxed at that band's rate.

Monthly payRate

NAPSA on gross pay

5% of gross pay, up to a monthly ceiling.

NHIMA on basic salary

1% of basic salary only, with no cap.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What's the difference between gross pay and basic salary?

Basic salary is your fixed pay for the job. Gross pay is basic salary plus allowances such as housing, transport and lunch, before anything is deducted. If your basic is K3,250 and your allowances add up to K3,250, your gross pay is K6,500.

Why doesn't my payslip match exactly?

The most common reason is NHIMA, which is charged on basic salary only. If you enter just your gross pay, the calculator treats all of it as basic. Choose "Basic + allowances" and enter both for the exact figure. Payslips can also include loans, union fees, insurance or a private pension, which this calculator does not know about.

Is NAPSA taken off before PAYE?

No. Since January 2025, employees' pension contributions no longer reduce taxable pay, so PAYE is worked out on your full gross. A real Zambian payslip checked against this calculator agreed to the ngwee.

Is my information saved or sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server. The only download is the day's exchange rates, when you open the currency converter. Links you choose to share do contain the amounts you entered.

Is this official?

No. It's an independent estimate, not tax advice and not affiliated with ZRA, NAPSA or NHIMA. Check anything important with ZRA or your payroll office.

When are the rates updated?

Every January, after the national budget sets the new PAYE bands and NAPSA publishes its new ceiling. Each figure's source is listed at the bottom of this page.

For developers

Use it in your own app

The same calculations are available as a free JSON API, for payroll tools, HR systems and job sites. Amounts come back as exact decimal strings, so there's no rounding to worry about.

  • GET /v1/take-home Full breakdown from gross or basic
  • GET /v1/required-salary Gross needed for a target take-home
  • POST /v1/compare Compare up to ten offers
  • GET /v1/rates/2026 Bands, rates and sources

What this assumes

  • PAYE is charged on gross pay. NAPSA and NHIMA are not deducted first.
  • NAPSA is 5% of gross pay, up to the monthly ceiling. NHIMA is 1% of basic salary, with no cap.
  • Annual figures are twelve equal months.
  • This is an estimate, not tax advice. Check anything important with ZRA or your payroll office.
